# Cadence

The language used for purchases, refunds and related learner communication in the course platform.

## Language

**Refund request**:
A learner's ask to get money back for a course purchase. It is Requested until a decision is made, then Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Refund (for the ask itself)

**Refund**:
Money actually returned to a learner for a course purchase. A Refund exists only once that money has been returned.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Chargeback**:
A dispute raised by a learner's bank or card issuer that forces a payment back. Reserved for that meaning only; this platform does not handle chargebacks.
_Avoid_: Refund, Refund request
